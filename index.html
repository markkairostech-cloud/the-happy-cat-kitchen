Welcome to Happy Cat

Food made with a
little bit of love.

And quite a lot of happiness.

🐾

The Happy Cat Kitchen creates beautiful, flavourful food for life's special moments. From private events and celebrations, to retreat dining and pop-up food experiences, we cook with fresh ingredients, big flavour and a genuine love for bringing people together.

Based in Penzance, Cornwall, we're inspired by the sea, the season and the simple joy of good food shared with great people.
